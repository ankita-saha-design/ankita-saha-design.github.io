SBMD : Single block multi debit

SBMD (Single Block, Multiple Debit) is a UPI payment option by Juspay that lets merchants block an amount once and debit it in parts as orders are fulfilled, while the customer's money keeps earning interest.

Made for merchants, the film frames the problem, introduces SBMD as the fix and shows it at work in real use cases. I developed the concept, storyboard and key visuals.
