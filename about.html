Ankita Saha is a visual designer and artist based in Bangalore, currently designing at Juspay. She holds a B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering from Amity University, Kolkata, and brings over four years of multidisciplinary craft to high-scale digital platforms.

She has shaped visual identities, design systems, and creative campaigns across tech and media for brands like Juspay, Pratilipi, and Flam. Ankita’s practice is rooted in structural clarity, visual precision, and scalable design that bridges engineering logic with striking aesthetics to create intuitive, memorable digital experiences.

Outside the canvas, you’ll usually find her traveling and documenting her life on the internet.
